The Mission

I am Dr. Shahadat Hussain, an engineer and researcher dedicated to the intersection of materials science, advanced manufacturing, and computational design.

My work is driven by a central question: How can we design internal structures to give materials extraordinary properties? From 3D-printed shape memory alloys to complex TPMS (Triply Periodic Minimal Surface) lattices, I explore the frontier of what is physically possible through additive manufacturing.

My Background

Currently, I serve as a Research Associate at New York University in Abu Dhabi. My journey in science and engineering has taken me from a PhD in Materials Science and Technology (AcSIR, India) to deep-diving into the mechanics of “smart” materials. I worked as a postdoctoral fellow at Khalifa University prior to the current affiliation.

Over the years, I’ve developed a specialized toolkit that includes:

  • Additive Manufacturing: Mastering Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) and FFF.

  • Smart Materials: Exploring the “memory” of NiTi and Cu-based alloys.

  • Computational Design: Using Python, SQL, and Data Science to predict how materials will behave before they are even printed.

Why I Started This Substack

The world of materials science can often feel locked behind paywalls and dense academic jargon. I created this space to pull back the curtain on:

  1. Technical Deep-Dives: Breaking down the latest trends in metamaterials and 3D printing.

  2. Research Insights: Sharing the “ah-ha” moments (and the failures) from my lab work at Khalifa University.

  3. The Digital Lab: Discussing how Data Science and Scientific Computing are changing the way we do engineering.

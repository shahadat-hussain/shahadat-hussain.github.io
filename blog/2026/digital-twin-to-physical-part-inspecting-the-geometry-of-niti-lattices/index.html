Why the Gyroid?

The Gyroid is a “Triply Periodic Minimal Surface” (TPMS). Unlike traditional strut-based lattices, the Gyroid features:

  • Continuous Curvature: This eliminates sharp corners where stress usually concentrates, leading to better fatigue life.

  • High Permeability: The open, interconnected pores are ideal for fluid flow (heat exchangers) and nutrient transport (biomedical scaffolds).

  • High Surface-to-Volume Ratio: This maximizes the functional area for shape memory effects.

From Mesh to Metal

Designing these structures requires specialized software (like MSLattice) and a deep understanding of how STL files translate to laser paths. By sharing this model, my goal is to provide a transparent look at the “Digital Twin” of the specimens used in my study, “Microstructural and surface analysis of NiTi TPMS lattice sections fabricated by laser powder bed fusion.”

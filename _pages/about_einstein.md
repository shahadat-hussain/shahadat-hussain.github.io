Dr. Shahadat Hussain is a materials scientist and mechanical engineer specializing in Additive Manufacturing (3D printing), computational design, and advanced functional materials. He currently conducts research as an Associate Research Scientist at New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD). His work integrates experimental fabrication with computational modeling to develop architected materials and metamaterials.

Dr. Hussain’s primary expertise lies in the design, fabrication, and characterization of architected materials and metamaterials. He specializes in developing complex Triply Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS) lattice structures, Interpenetrating Phase Composites (IPCs), and cellular materials tailored for high-performance applications. His experimental work encompasses a wide spectrum of advanced manufacturing techniques, including Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) for metals, Fused Filament Fabrication (FFF) for composites, and Stereolithography (SLA) for precision patterns. He possesses domain knowledge in working with Shape Memory Alloys (such as NiTi), green steel manufacturing pathways, and fiber-reinforced polymers.

Dr. Hussain holds a Ph.D. in Materials Science and Technology from the Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR), India, where his foundational research focused on high-temperature shape memory alloys. He previously served as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Khalifa University, further expanding his expertise in the additive manufacturing of smart materials.
